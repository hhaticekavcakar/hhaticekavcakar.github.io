IE 3524 Integrated Manufacturing Systems

Converting Process Analysis and Optimization – Alkim Kağıt

Analyzed the paper slitting process at Alkim Kağıt, focusing on trim loss, setup time and cutting-pattern efficiency. Built an Excel Solver linear programming model and used a 2×2 Design of Experiments framework to evaluate cutting and sequencing strategies.

4.50% → 4.20%

  • Analyzed the paper slitting process at Alkim Kağıt.
  • Focused on trim loss, setup time and cutting-pattern efficiency.
